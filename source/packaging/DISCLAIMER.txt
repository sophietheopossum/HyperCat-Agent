WHAT IT DOES ON YOUR MACHINE
  • Runs a local UI plus background worker processes, all under YOUR user account.
  • Stores its data locally under  ~/.local/share/hypercat  (conversations,
    projects, sessions, memory). Nothing is uploaded to us.
  • Makes OUTBOUND NETWORK CONNECTIONS to your configured LLM provider
    (OpenRouter by default, https://openrouter.ai) to answer prompts. Your
    prompts and the agents' work are sent to that provider under their terms and
    privacy policy. An egress allowlist restricts where it may connect.
  • Requires an API key you supply, either through the OPENROUTER_API_KEY
    environment variable or in the application. The key is held in memory or,
    if you choose to save it, your operating system's keyring; HyperCat never
    writes it to a plaintext config file and never passes it on a command line.

NOT FOR: production workloads, untrusted multi-user machines, or processing data
you are not permitted to send to a third-party LLM provider.

By installing and running this build you acknowledge the above.
